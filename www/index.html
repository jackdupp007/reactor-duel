Reactor Duel

You don't fly the ship. You run its reactor. Ten units of power, three systems, and power takes time to move. You have until the ships close to weapons range to set up. Read the enemy, then commit.

  • ShieldsMore power means a bigger shield, faster regen and more damage resistance. A broken shield reboots faster with power in it, and stalls with none.
  • WeaponsThe pulse laser turret tracks the enemy and scales up to 3 power. The rail cannon needs 4+ power, only fires straight ahead, and punches through shields. Your pilot makes attack runs to line it up.
  • EnginesMore power means more evasion and sharper turns, so your pilot lines up the rail faster and stays out of the enemy's torpedo arc. Engine power also charges the jump drive. Press Jump to escape, but you leave the salvage behind.
