Reactor Duel

You're the captain, and you run the reactor. Ten units of power, three systems, and power takes time to move. Your crew will tell you what they need. Who gets it is up to you.

  • T
    Tally, comms. Tells you what the enemy is up to. Relays your orders.
  • F
    Fergus, engineer. Runs the shields and wants power for them.
  • D
    Dot, weapons. Laser turret, plus a rail cannon called Doris that needs 4 power.
  • J
    Juno, pilot. Engines mean dodging, lining up Doris, and charging the jump drive.

When things go wrong, hold Get us out of here once the jump drive is warm. You'll lose the salvage, but keep the ship.
